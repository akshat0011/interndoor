The gist

As a data engineer, you'll design and maintain scalable data systems, transforming raw data into usable formats for analysis. You'll work closely with technology teams to build data solutions that support the bank's strategic goals. This role suits someone passionate about data engineering and eager to contribute to impactful projects.

What you would actually do

  • Build, maintain, test and optimise scalable data architecture
  • Extract, transform, and load data for analysts and scientists
  • Collaborate with core tech teams on data solutions
  • Advocate for data structure improvements
