The gist

As a Technology Analyst, you'll translate business needs into technical solutions, working with global teams to ensure secure and efficient systems. You'll analyze complex data dependencies, use AI tools to generate requirements, and collaborate across departments to deliver impactful results. This role suits someone with technical analysis skills and a passion for solving business challenges through technology.

What you would actually do

  • Analyzes data mappings and dependencies across Java Spring Boot Microservices, Guidewire PolicyCenter, and other platforms
  • Collaborates with business and engineering teams to translate outcomes into technical requirements
  • Uses AI tools like GitHub Copilot to research and generate technical requirements
  • Works in agile environment with global teams, flexible hours, and hybrid location options
