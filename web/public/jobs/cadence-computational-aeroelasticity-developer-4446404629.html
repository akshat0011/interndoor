The gist

This role involves developing advanced aeroelastic simulation tools for high-speed and hypersonic flight applications. The candidate will work on computational methods, fluid-structure interaction, and software development for aerospace systems. Ideal for recent graduates with research experience in computational aeroelasticity or related fields.

What you would actually do

  • Develop aeroelastic simulation tools for subsonic to hypersonic flight
  • Use C++ and Python for scientific software and HPC optimization
  • Collaborate with teams on multidisciplinary aerospace simulations
