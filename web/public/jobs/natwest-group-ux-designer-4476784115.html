The gist

As a UX Designer, you will shape digital experiences for customers by designing websites and applications that are intuitive and efficient. You will collaborate with product teams to develop features and ensure consistency across platforms. This role is ideal for someone with a strong portfolio and experience in user-centered design tools like Figma and Axure.

What you would actually do

  • Design websites and apps for seamless customer experiences across desktop and mobile
  • Work with product teams to create new features and improve existing products
  • Create wireframes, mock-ups, and prototypes for user-centered solutions
