Where this is kept

On this device, in this browser, and nowhere else. Your application list is never sent to InternDoor and we cannot see it. There is no account and nothing to sign up for.

That has a real cost, and it is worth knowing before you rely on it: the list will not follow you to another browser or another phone, and clearing your browsing data will delete it. Take a backup file if the list matters to you.

Backup and restore

The backup is a plain JSON file. Restoring merges it with whatever is already here and keeps the newer copy of any role that is in both, so restoring an old backup never throws away newer applications.
