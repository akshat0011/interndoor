What people write about

  • A listing that is wrong, expired or duplicated. Send the page’s address and what is wrong with it — that is enough to find the row behind it. Report a listing.
  • Employers, to take a posting down. It comes down, and the page is replaced by a link to your other open roles rather than left to 404. Ask for a removal.
  • Employers, to be picked up automatically. Point us at the careers page you post on and new internships are found from it within about half an hour of going live. There is no charge for this and no listing fee.
  • Colleges and placement cells. The board is free for your students and needs no signup, so there is nothing to sign. If you want the live openings in a form you can circulate, say which branches and years. Write about a placement cell.
  • Anything else — a bug, a wrong summary, a question about where a number on the numbers page came from.

What this is, and is not

InternDoor is not a recruiter and not a placement agency. We do not take applications, forward resumes, or charge students or employers anything. Every listing links back to the employer’s own posting, and that posting is where you apply and the source of truth.

We are not affiliated with the companies listed here. Writing to this address does not reach them, and we cannot tell you where your application stands — only the employer can.

There is no account to close and no profile to delete: nothing on this site is tied to a person. Your saved applications live in your own browser, which the tracker page explains in full.
