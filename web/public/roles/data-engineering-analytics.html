Data engineering and data analyst in Bengaluru

What the work is

Data engineers build the pipelines and warehouses that move and store a company’s data; data analysts query it to answer business questions. SQL is the common ground, with Python, Spark and a cloud data platform on the engineering side and dashboards and reporting on the analyst side.
