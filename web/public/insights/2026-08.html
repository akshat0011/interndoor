Engineering internships in India: August 2026

In August 2026 we recorded 333 engineering internships in India from 132 employers. Only 23 of the 333 stated a stipend.

August 2026 · 333 postings · 132 employers · compiled on 7 October 2026. These figures will not change.

Only 23 of 333 postings stated a stipend.

How this was measured

We track engineering internships as they are published on public job boards and on companies' own careers pages, and record each one the first time we see it. This report counts every internship we first recorded in India between 1 August 2026 and 31 August 2026; entry-level full-time roles are not counted.

A posting only reaches the board if its employer is on a list we keep of companies we believe are real and actually pay interns. The list grows as we add employers, so a bigger total from one month to the next can mean a bigger list rather than a bigger market. Employers are counted as they stood on that list when this report was compiled.

What these numbers are not

  • Not a census of every internship in India. It covers the employers that pass the gate above.
  • “Did not state a stipend” does not mean unpaid. It means the posting carried no figure we could read.
  • Applicant counts are the posting's own figure at the moment we found it, not a final count.
  • Skills, city and work mode are read from the posting text, so something the posting does not mention is missing rather than absent.
