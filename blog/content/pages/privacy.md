## Page views

The blog counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics. It does not use cookies. Visitors are counted by a hash of the incoming request, which is discarded after 24 hours, and what is kept is aggregate: which pages were read, where readers came from, and the kind of device and browser. It cannot tell us who you are.

## Your theme

If you switch between the dark and light theme, the choice is saved in your own browser so the next page opens the same way. It is never sent to us.

## The daily email

If you subscribe, your email address is sent to InternDoor's email provider, Buttondown, together with your IP address as a record of the signup. It is used only to send the daily email of new roles: at most one a day, and only on days with new roles. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To be removed by hand, write to [akshat@interndoor.com](mailto:akshat@interndoor.com).

## Links out

The share buttons are ordinary links to LinkedIn, WhatsApp and X. Nothing is sent to those services until you click one, and their own privacy policies apply once you do. Links in posts to InternDoor and to other sites are ordinary links too.
